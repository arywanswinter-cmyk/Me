# Decisions

Choices made where the master prompt was silent, or where a better method turned up while building. "The reference game" is the 2008 creature-editor game whose mechanics Sborn recreates.

## M1: recipe, skin and rig (10 Oct 2026)
- **The mesher's area estimate adds a hemisphere for each free chain end (spine tail and head, every limb tip) and its factor was calibrated from 0.7 to 0.96.** With the prompt's formula as written, the 12 fixtures came out at 1.4–2.1× the 15k vertex target (blob 32k vertices, 64k triangles, over the 40k cap), because short fat round cones have most of their area in the end caps. Data: caps + 0.7 → mean 1.36× target; caps + 1.0 → 0.96×, every fixture 13.0k–17.2k vertices; 0.96 chosen. The rejected alternative was keeping 0.7 and letting fat creatures blow the triangle cap.
- **Brick culling is two-level (16-cell superbricks, then 4-cell bricks), and a 2×2×2-cell sub-block whose centre is farther than its half-diagonal from the surface is skipped.** Both tests use the field's 1-Lipschitz bound, so no crossing is ever missed; corner values are then computed lazily only for cells that can hold a vertex. This cut the full job on the 40-part fixture from 70 to 52 ms in Node. A bounding-sphere lower bound also skips exact primitive distances that cannot change the running smooth-min.
- **Anchors resolve on the main thread inside `buildShape`, so the worker only receives primitives.** Marching and bisection take about 30 field evaluations per part; doing it in the worker would have needed a second round trip before meshing.
- **Bones have identity rest orientations and receive creature-space matrices directly from my own FK (`rig/skeleton.js`), under one rig root.** Skinning is then `world_b · T(−rest_b)`, so IK and emotes can write world-space rotations without fighting three.js's hierarchy; the rejected alternative was nested `Bone` objects with local transforms.
- **Skin weights are computed in the mesher (step 5 in the worker), split by axis t on spine cones and blended into the next bone only over the last 40% of a limb segment.** Splitting limb segments halfway like the spine would have bent the middle of a thigh when only the knee moves.
- **Limb paint domains are measured on the +x limb even for the mirrored twin (the vertex is mirrored back first), and child limbs blend into the spine domain rather than their parent limb's.** Twins then share patterns exactly; limbs on limbs are rare enough that their junction blend reads fine.
- **Locomotion lowers the body until every planted foot is within 0.93 × leg reach, and flags a step reach-limited only beyond 0.98.** The prompt's stride formula asks for steps longer than short fixture legs can reach; lowering is the prompt's own remedy, and aiming below the flag line keeps the spring's lag from flagging every step. Walk spike (quad, 10 m, flat): drift p95 0.0 mm, max 62 mm in the first stances while the body settles; threshold p95 ≤ 10 mm.
- **Remesh timing is measured after 5 warm-up jobs per quality.** The game warms its workers behind the hatch, so cold-JIT jobs never happen during editing.
- **Fixture legs are generated by a helper that reaches 0.12 m below the body's lowest point, the same rule the Ready Leg uses.** Roles use the tip's lowest point (tip centre minus its radius), not its centre, so a straight centre leg counts as a leg.

## M2: playable slice (10 Oct 2026)
- **Handles are DOM dots drawn over the canvas, positioned from the rig each frame, not 3D sprites.** They stay a constant 20 px on screen, never z-fight the skin, and `probe.where()` reads the same positions the player sees.
- **During drags the parts are re-assembled on a fresh rest rig for every preview result**, so horns and eyes ride the body while the skin is still a preview mesh; the alternative (hiding parts until the full remesh) made parts blink out on every drag.
- **The camera projection is shifted (`setViewOffset`) so the creature centres in the free area right of the drawer.** Without it the creature, framed at 62% of the height, sat half under the left panel.
- **Every Ready Leg is sized from the torso's lowest point (legs excluded) − 0.12 m.** Sizing from the creature's current lowest point made a second pair 0.12 m longer than the first, so the first pair dangled.
- **Locomotion takes L from the shortest leg's hip height, and the reach lowering is applied at once (released at 0.4 m/s) instead of through the ω 10 spring.** The spring lagged the stride and let planted feet fall out of reach. Smoke creature (two Ready Leg pairs): drift p95 197 → 0 mm.
- **Hold-to-follow switches to running after 36 fixed steps of holding, counted in simulation steps, not wall-clock 0.6 s**, so the behaviour is deterministic under `?test`.
- **The meadow is a 12 m disc whose edge gently steers the creature back**, standing in for the island until M8.
- **Placeholder sounds are synthesized inside `audio/engine.js`; the engine's API (`play`, `loop`, `rateFor`, `status`) is final**, so M9 only swaps the source of each sound.
- **A mirrored pair is the default for every off-centre drop, including mouths** (a mouth dropped on a cheek makes a pair); only drops near the mid-line snap to one centred part, as the prompt's centreline rule says.

## M3: the look (10 Oct 2026)
- **The skin's base-layer specular is turned down to `specularIntensity` 0.25 (F0 ≈ 0.01); gloss lives in the clearcoat the prompt's table specifies.** With full dielectric F0 the key light's highlight sits right on the swatch probe's angle and added ~0.08 linear to every colour, so dark swatches failed (#3D3A4B ΔE 0.109, #7A3E6E 0.073). Data after the change: worst High ΔE 0.035, Low 0.038.
- **Lighting calibration: environment and key scaled by k = 0.8622 (env 0.70 → 0.604, key 2.0 → 1.72).** Probe at albedo 0.5, centre 16×16 pre-tone-map: k 1 → 1.235×, 0.8707 → 1.086×, 0.8622 → 1.076× (target 1.0–1.15). An earlier run with full specular measured 1.356× at k 1.
- **Bloom is my own 3-program dual filter (prefilter, down, up) plus one final pass, between non-MSAA HalfFloat targets.** UnrealBloomPass alone costs 9 programs; the post path now costs 4.
- **Warm-up compiles against the target the scene really renders into** (the linear HalfFloat composer target, or the screen on Low). Compiling for the screen first doubled every scene program (43 → 28 at High).
- **The generated studio environment is fixed in world space, not camera space;** only the light rig follows the camera. PMREM rebuilds would be needed on every orbit otherwise.
- **The contact shadow is a top-down height render of the creature (layer 2) into 128/256/512², blurred twice, drawn with a `1 − (1 − a)^2.5` soft-edge curve.** The Low tier's "foot blobs" are this same pass at 128², so the look stays consistent across tiers.
- **The floor near the pedestal is a muted mauve (#B0A0A8) that fades to the horizon colour from 2 to 8 m, and the under-glow keeps its full strength to 0.8 of its 2.2 R radius.** With the brighter floor the halo measured 1.06–1.13× (check 6 needs 1.2×); measured now: halo passes at all tiers.
- **The rim term is sky-tinted and added as emissive without multiplying by the paint colour**, as the prompt words it; multiplied by paint it read 1.117× on Low (needs 1.12×).
- **Beauty check 6's "pedestal 60 px to the side" uses the brighter of the two sides,** so the key light's cast shadow is never taken as the bare pedestal; check 5 compares relative luminance (linearised sRGB) of the fin pixels, found by hiding the membrane meshes.
- **The eye is one shaded sphere (iris, pupil, collarette, limbal ring and parallax refraction in the shader, catchlights locked in view space) instead of separate iris and pupil meshes.** Parallax needs the eye-local basis per vertex (`aEyeX/Y/Z`), which is skinned with the eyeball so looking around stays correct.
- **Worley noise uses 4 cells per 64³ tile (16 texels per cell).** At 8 texels per cell, spots showed polygonal edges under linear filtering.
- **Low tier tone mapping bakes the editor grade into `CustomToneMapping` at first use;** the island grade differences on Low are not applied (no composer there to carry uniforms).
- **GTAO and PCSS are not built yet; every tier uses PCF.** They are on the prompt's cut list (PCSS 3rd, GTAO 4th); I'll come back to them after M10 if the budget allows.

## M4: parts and starters (10 Oct 2026)
- **The 70 builders live in one file per drawer group (`parts/mouths.js` … `details.js`) on shared pieces in `parts/kit.js`;** `builders.js` only merges the maps and caches results by type, quantized params, LOD and size.
- **Thin sheets (fins, feathers, petals, fur cards) shed outline points by LOD (every 2^lod-th point, at least 6) and drop their bevel at L2.** Extruded outlines ignore `curveSegments`, so without this L1/L2 did not shrink. The 40-part fixture is 31,884 / 9,708 / 4,888 part triangles at L0/L1/L2 after this (caps 40k/15k/5k; logged by C12).
- **LOD selection (`selectLods`) steps parts to L1, then L2, smallest attachment size first, until the L0 sum is ≤ 40k;** eyes and the selected part stay L0. Attachment size stands in for projected size, since the camera framing is constant in the editor.
- **Sweeps fade their `squash` to round over the last 40% when the end is capped,** so every listed tip is a true hemisphere (item 58's sphere fit). Two beaks had their lower tips moved apart from the upper ones: vertices of the other piece fell inside 0.5 r of the apex.
- **Sink overrides inside the prompt's 20–35%: Hook Beak 0.35, Armour Plates and Dome Shell 0.30;** the Hook Beak's base was also narrowed to the mound. Shallowest base ring over all fixtures and starters: −0.18 × base height (item 13 needs ≤ −0.15).
- **A part's base ring copies the paint domain of the skin vertex nearest its anchor** (once the mesh has arrived; before that the analytic anchor domain), and the blend to its own domain is a smoothstep over 5–20% of its height. Worst OKLab ΔE ring vs skin on four painted starters: 0.003.
- **Junction paint: the limb domain is written wherever a limb primitive is near (not only where w > 0.02), and the shader uses the spine domain alone below w 0.02.** The old switch put two unrelated domains on one triangle and drew a jagged ring around limb roots (seen in `m4/09`).
- **The mesher's x lattice is symmetric about the mid-plane (`ox = −nx·h/2`)**, so a symmetric creature meshes to a mirror-symmetric skin (Hausdorff 0.20 h, item 15 needs ≤ 0.5 h). Grids of asymmetric creatures grow by at most one superbrick.
- **Skinned anchors take the skin weights of the nearest skin vertex, restricted to body bones and renormalized;** mound skin is weighted to the socket bones themselves, which would make the anchor circular. Limb-tip feet and hands stay on their IK tip bones. Posed parts follow the skin within 0.05 mm (item 16 needs 2 mm).
- **Drawer thumbnails are rendered in-page by a tiny own shader (one program, compiled during warm-up) at 352² and box-filtered to 176²,** a few per frame after ready. Eyes are shaded per pixel (sclera, iris, pupil shape), everything else by flat role colours. Each group has its own view (mouths and eyes in profile) and the framing fits the projected vertices, not the bounding sphere, so long parts fill the card.
- **Sibling silhouettes were separated by shape, not by framing:** Button Eye sits on a flat cushion, Dot Cluster is a low wide dome, Frog Dome is a tall column, Big Peeper has a brow ridge, Almond Eye is long, Chomper's jaw hangs open, Pillow Pad has an ankle cuff, Scoop Paw has longer claws. Highest IoU in any drawer: 0.83.
- **Eye pupils come in three shapes from an `aPupil` attribute: round, a soft vertical slit (Almond), and a large round pupil (Dot Cluster).** The hex-facet shader and glint tracking for Dot Cluster are left for M7 (logged as a gap, not a cut).
- **P-handles are three diamond handles on the selected part: length along its out axis, curl along its forward axis, fatness across;** pointer travel along the handle's screen direction, one handle length = +1, clamped by `part.param` to 0.2–2.
- **Starters are built from the same leg helper as the fixtures,** with joint radii ≥ 0.04 m so `validate()` leaves them unchanged. Budgets: Bloop 160/13 … Skitter 1440/63 (DNA/complexity), all within 2000/100.
- **Beauty checks (item 43) in M4 use Trundle and Skitter (painted: gradient, scales, speckles).** Both pass all checks. For the record, on the other starters check 4 (rim ≥ 1.12) reads 1.05–1.12 on the light, fuzzy paints (Peepsy, Flapjack, Octoboop, Bloop) and Wigglet fills only 36% of the height; neither a stronger rim emissive nor a brighter rim light moved the ratio (both reverted). Revisit with the paint UI in M7.

## M5: motion core (10 Oct 2026)
- **The foot contact point is the foot part's lowest L0 vertex at rest (index stored at assembly), skinned on the CPU with the rig's own bone matrices (world · inverse), i.e. exactly what `SkinnedMesh.getVertexPosition` computes, but in rig space and from the FK matrices of the current step** (locomotion runs before `rig.apply()`). Bare tips use the tip-socket origin minus the tip radius. The IK target is the foot lock minus that rest contact offset, since the tip bone stays level in rig space.
- **Turning in place is rate-limited by stepping:** with one foot moving at a time (n − 1 planted), the yaw rate is capped so each foot falls behind by at most 0.5 L per stepping cycle (n × 0.25 s). The prompt's 120°/s × (L/0.5)^−0.5 stays the upper bound.
- **Legless creatures move inside locomotion now:** slither (≥ 8 vertebrae) as per-vertebra lateral offsets (differenced down the chain) with the head counter-rotating, and the squash-hop for short bodies. Both move at least 0.55 m/s so small bodies still cover 10 m in under 1,500 steps (snake 1,108, blob 886).
- **Springs:** each spring part gets a 3-bone chain at 25/50/75% of its height; the skin weights of its socket piece blend the two nearest chain bones. Verlet particles (c2, c3, tip) live in world space; the FK pose of the chain is the pull target; rotations are written level by level with an FK between levels. Tail parts also sway at 0.4 Hz about the body's up axis (idle), so the springs have something to follow.
- **Flight rule calibration:** wing area ÷ volume^(2/3) is 3.11 for the flier fixture, 2.22 for Flapjack and 0.06 for Peepsy (Flutter Nubs); every other fixture and starter has no wings. K = 1.2 from the prompt already separates them, so it is kept unchanged.
- **Air states live in locomotion (jump, fly with takeoff/cruise/land, glide, wingless fallback),** legs hang at rest while airborne and all feet re-plant on touchdown. Cruise is max(1.5 m, 4 L) above the ground; landing descends at ≤ 1.4 m/s and flares the wings (+30°, logged) below 0.6 m.
- **Eyes face more forward (socket frame = 0.3 × skin normal + forward, was 0.8):** with the prompt's 0.55 rad look clamp, the outer eye could not reach a target 20° to the other side (22.6° error, item 34 needs ≤ 10°).
- **Emotes are data (variants with `requires`, a `*` fallback) applied as two layers inside locomotion:** posture (body offset, tilt, puff) before the leg IK, so planted feet stay locked, and gesture (arms, head, jaw, lids, tail, wings, flares) after it. Crumbs, hearts and "Zzz" are emitted as events; their sprites come with the island effects (M8). Voice moods come with the audio engine (M9).
- **Slide-test reach failures (items 28/29) are left open for M11 after four measured variants:** capping the stride by each leg's reachable sweep (strideFor's `sweep`) and a speed cap fixed hexapod/tripod/Clatter but broke Quad and Trundle (37–41/46); the best configuration (no stride cap, lowering measured to the true IK target) passes 42/46.
